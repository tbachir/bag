## Why Diátaxis?

Tutorials teach, how-to guides support practical work, reference pages provide facts and explanations provide context. They describe **types of help**, not a fixed filesystem taxonomy.

## Why Fumadocs navigation?

Its Page Tree and optional `meta.json` already support nested directories, ordering and subnavigation. Keep them native and avoid a redundant navigation compiler.

## Why an upstream CLI?

Using the official generator reduces the burden of maintaining Next.js and Fumadocs version wiring. The added examples demonstrate conventions without replacing the upstream framework.
