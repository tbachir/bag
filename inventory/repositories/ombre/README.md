## Why keep it?

Ombre captures text or voice, formalizes incoming work into cards, and applies deterministic risk, confidence, and delegation gates.

## What to extract or study

Study `src/lib/engine`, the append-only decision journal and charter constraints for safe agent-delegation patterns.

## Trade-offs & safety

The README states that V1 has no execution agent wired and caps actions at preparation. Do not describe it as autonomous production execution.
