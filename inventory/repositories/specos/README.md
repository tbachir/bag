## Why keep it?

SpecOS organizes canonical specifications, contracts, work packages, tasks, readiness rules, architecture decisions, and Fumadocs-ready documentation.

## What to extract or study

Useful as a blueprint for specification-first agent workflows. Study `SPECOS-1.0.md`, `specos.yaml`, `templates/`, `contracts/`, and `rules/readiness.yaml` before adapting.

## Trade-offs & safety

Do not transplant its enterprise maturity defaults or approval gates indiscriminately; tailor them to project size.
