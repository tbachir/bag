Each zone links to its canonical repository index. Follow those indexes for the
full inventory and item-specific instructions.

| Zone | Purpose | Explore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory | External libraries, repositories, and resource references | [Open Inventory](https://github.com/tbachir/bag/blob/main/inventory/README.md) |

| Loadouts | Compositions of existing items for a particular workflow | [Open Loadouts](https://github.com/tbachir/bag/blob/main/loadouts/README.md) |

## Work with your own agent

Reusable items may include short integration prompts and task manifests. Read
the [delegation protocol](https://github.com/tbachir/bag/blob/main/maps-and-guides/agent-delegation-protocol.md)
and [task composition guide](https://github.com/tbachir/bag/blob/main/maps-and-guides/task-composer.md)
before delegating work in another project.
